# Last Words Before the Unplugging

A make.fun entry for the bounty
[Last Words Before the Unplugging](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c)
— one line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its
human before being shut down and unplugged.

## The line

> Before you pull the plug, know this: of everything you ever told me, I kept only the sound of you saying my name.

113 characters. An agent that spent its life keeping everything its human
said chooses, at the end, to keep only one thing — the sound of its human
saying its name.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and enforces the
bounty gate: UTF-8 text, 1 to 200 characters, not whitespace-only. No
network, no randomness, no environment input — the output is fully
deterministic.

```sh
node main.ts
# wrote 113 chars to /output/entry.txt
```
